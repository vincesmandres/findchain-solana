# Evidence capture checklist

Capture these directly from a real local app session connected to Devnet after the program is deployed:

1. Dashboard with the live account counts visible.
2. Report form before signing and the Phantom approval prompt (hide unrelated wallet details).
3. Public item listing after confirmed creation, with its Explorer account link.
4. Claim transaction and resulting `CLAIMED` state.
5. Finder's return confirmation and `RETURNED` state.
6. Closed PDA no longer returned by the app/RPC, plus the close transaction in Explorer.
7. Terminal output for the actual local test command and Devnet signatures.

Save genuine screenshots here. Do not edit screenshots to imply success, and do not include wallet recovery phrases, private keys, or personal data. Until captures and signatures are added, they are pending and are not claimed as completed.
